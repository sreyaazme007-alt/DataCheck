🛡 Enforced Schema & Rules

DataCheck uses deterministic rule algorithms to evaluate every row against strict constraints:

  • Student_ID Primary Key & Unique. Must be a positive integer; duplicate keys are flagged.
  • Full_Name Mandatory. Cannot be empty, null, or contain illegal characters.
  • Email RFC-822 Regex Format. Validates syntax (user@domain.tld).
  • Age Range (16 – 100). Integer type and domain bounds check.
  • Department Permitted Enum. Allowed: CSE, ECE, IT, MECH, CIVIL, AIDS.
  • CGPA Range (0.00 – 10.00). Numeric floating-point check.
  • Enrollment_Date ISO 8601 Date. Enforces valid YYYY-MM-DD calendar dates.
  • Deduplication Full Row Hashing. Identifies duplicate rows across the entire dataset.
